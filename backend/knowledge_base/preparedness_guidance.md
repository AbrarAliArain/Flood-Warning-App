# Flood Preparedness & Response Guidance (Sindh)

Compiled guidance curated for the AquaShield demo from NDMA/PDMA public advisories
and the project research documents. Curation: DEMO.

## Pre-monsoon drainage preparation

Clean storm drains, nullahs and cross-drainage works before the monsoon window
(July-September). In urban districts such as Hyderabad and Karachi, paved-surface
runoff overwhelms stormwater systems; pre-position de-watering pumps at known
waterlogging hotspots and verify pump stations operate.

## Embankment and river gauge watch

Where risk is riverine (Matiari, Sujawal, Thatta, Ghotki), assign breach-watch
volunteers along protective embankments when gauge flows cross the High flood
level. Monitor Manchar Lake containment near Dadu and Jamshoro during very-high
flows, as in 2022 when banks were deliberately breached.

## Community early warning

Disseminate alerts through union-council volunteers, mosque loudspeakers, SMS and
radio. Pre-agree evacuation routes and camp sites for low-lying settlements;
prioritize warnings when rainfall intensity exceeds 50-100 mm in a short spell,
the threshold disaster authorities associate with urban flooding in Hyderabad.

## Protecting vulnerable facilities

Raise and waterproof records, medicines and equipment in schools, hospitals and
relief warehouses in high-risk zones. Stock clean water, ORS and chlorine tablets
to counter waterborne disease after standing water events.

## Drainage congestion response (LBOD zone)

In LBOD-served districts (Badin, Sanghar, Shaheed Benazirabad, Naushahro Feroze)
clear regulator gates and remove encroachments from drains before peak rain;
the system is documented as unable to cope with climate-driven heavy rainfall.

## Post-flood response and recovery

After water recedes, dewater stagnant areas quickly (standing water persisted
months in Naushahro Feroze and Mirpur Khas after 2022), assess house damage for
relief allocation, and inspect roads and underpasses before reopening.
